It reduces what an agent can reach

It does not make running untrusted code safe. HypDMZ puts layers between a swarm and the network: an egress floor keyed on who sends, an allowlisting proxy, vaulted credentials, hourly caps and an audit trail. Each layer can be misconfigured, and each can have a flaw nobody has found yet. A container shares the host's kernel, so a kernel or runtime vulnerability can cross it.

A passing egress check shows that its probes were blocked, not that no bypass exists

hypdmz egress-check runs a fixed set of escape attempts from the swarm's own seat. A route it does not try is a route it does not test.

Tested where it says, unverified elsewhere

The protections described on hypdmz.com were checked on the configurations named there, and the page lists its own limits. Other hosts, versions and policies are unverified. HypDMZ is alpha software and has not had an independent security audit.

You remain responsible

That covers your keys, your firewall policy and what it allows, what your agents do with the access you give them, and your compliance with your model provider's terms and your own obligations. Keep a spending cap with your model provider: the hourly caps count calls and bytes, not dollars.
